## How each point was verified

Names, codes and seats of Indonesian kabupaten and kota come from Kepmendagri No 300.2.2-2430 Tahun 2025. A name must match the Wikidata item that carries the same administrative code (P2588), or the geoBoundaries polygon when Wikidata has no label.

The point is the regent's or mayor's office when OpenStreetMap maps one inside the regency polygon (sanity limit 30 km to any other source). Otherwise it is the point the most independent source groups agree on within 10 km, and at least two groups must agree. GeoNames, Wikidata, Kepmendagri and OpenStreetMap each count once; a Wikidata coordinate identical to the Kepmendagri one counts as the same group, because one copied the other. Every point must lie inside its own geoBoundaries ADM2 polygon (tolerance 1 km).

Countries are placed at the GeoNames capital. It must agree within 25 km with Wikidata's current capital or Natural Earth's capital point, and lie inside the country's Natural Earth map unit (tolerance 5 km).
